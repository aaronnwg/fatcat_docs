# Welcome

FatCat is a non-custodial trading platform built for messaging apps — currently available in Telegram. It brings the best Solana trading experience right into your hands. You stay in control of your funds the whole time.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Never share your seed phrase or private key. FatCat support will never ask for them.
{% endhint %}

### What is FatCat Bot?

FatCat runs inside Telegram. You sign in with your email and a secure Solana wallet is created automatically.

You can:

* Swap Solana tokens at market price.
* Place limit orders.
* Trade Perpetual Futures (Perps).
* Automate buys and sells with DCA.

#### How it works
FatCat uses Privy embedded wallets: your private key is generated inside a hardware-isolated environment, split into encrypted shares, and never stored as a whole. Neither FatCat nor Privy can access it. FatCat only prepares transactions — nothing executes without your approval.

When you set up a transaction, FatCat prepares the transaction and you approve it directly inside the app. Nothing executes without your explicit approval.


{% hint style="warning" %}
Perps are high risk, especially at high leverage.
{% endhint %}
