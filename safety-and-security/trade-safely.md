# Trade Safely

{% hint style="warning" %}
Never share your seed phrase or private key. FatCat support will never ask for them.
{% endhint %}

### Protect your wallet

* Keep your private key and seed phrase safe — never share them.
* Lock your devices and review every wallet prompt before you sign.

### Verify what you're trading

* Confirm the token mint address from an official source — watch for copycats with the same name or ticker. You can check a mint on a trusted explorer like <https://solscan.io>.
* Make sure liquidity and volume are real and sufficient for your size; expect slippage on thin pools.
* Review price impact before you submit.

### If something feels off

Stop and don't sign — cancel the transaction. If you suspect compromise, move remaining funds to a fresh wallet and disconnect anything unfamiliar.

See also: [Where to Find Tokens](https://docs.fatcatbot.io/tokens/where-to-find-tokens) · [Avoiding Scams](https://docs.fatcatbot.io/safety-and-security/avoiding-scams).
