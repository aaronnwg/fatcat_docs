# Active DCA Orders

**Button:** `🗓️ Active DCA Orders` (DCA Menu)

Open `⏰ DCA` → `🗓️ Active DCA Orders` to view progress and per-order details, and to cancel an order. To cancel, tap the order, tap **Cancel**, and confirm — remaining uncommitted funds are returned to your wallet.

{% hint style="info" %}
Cancelling stops **future** executions only. Already-executed swaps and already-collected fees are not reversed.
{% endhint %}

If your list looks empty or a cancelled order still shows, confirm you're on the right wallet and refresh after a short delay (indexing can lag). DCA orders live on Jupiter's recurring program — you can also manage them at <https://jup.ag/recurring>.
