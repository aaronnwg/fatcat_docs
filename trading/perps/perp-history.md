# Perp History

**Button:** `📚 Perp History` (Perps menu) · Command: `/historical_positions`

Perp History shows your position events in reverse-chronological order — opens, closes, trigger closes (SL/TP), and liquidations. Closed positions show realized PnL, and FatCat generates a shareable PnL card you can use to share results.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The PnL shown includes venue close and borrow fees but does **not** include FatCat's open fee or the venue's opening fee. Full breakdown: [Fee Structure](https://docs.fatcatbot.io/fees-and-rewards/fee-structure).
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="info" %}
If your latest event isn't showing, reopen `📚 Perp History` after a short delay — on-chain finality and indexing can take a moment during congestion.
{% endhint %}
