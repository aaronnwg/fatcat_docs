# Getting Started

Before you can trade, set up your wallet and fund it with SOL.

FatCat Bot uses Privy embedded wallets — your Solana wallet is created automatically when you sign in with your email, all through Telegram. Neither FatCat nor Privy can access your private key; only you can authorize transactions.

* [Setting Up Your Wallet](https://docs.fatcatbot.io/overview/getting-started/setting-up-your-wallet) — create your wallet and understand how it's secured.
* [Funding Your Wallet](https://docs.fatcatbot.io/overview/getting-started/funding-your-wallet) — deposit SOL to start trading.
