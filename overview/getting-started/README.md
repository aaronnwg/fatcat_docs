# Getting Started

Before you can trade, you need to set up your wallet and fund it with SOL.

FatCat Bot supports Privy embedded wallets and wallet connection. Instead of downloading a wallet app or browser extension, you can create a Solana wallet when you sign in with your email or phone number, or connect an existing wallet. The entire experience happens inside Telegram.

Your private key is generated and secured entirely within Privy’s hardware-isolated environment. FatCat never has access to your key. Privy cannot access your key either. Only you, through your authenticated session, can authorize transactions.

### In this section

* [Setting Up Your Wallet](setting-up-your-wallet.md): connect or create your wallet and understand how embedded wallets are secured.
* [Funding Your Wallet](funding-your-wallet.md): deposit SOL so you can start trading.
