# LINE

FatCat is available in LINE.

In LINE, you trade through the [AI Assistant](../../ai-assistant/ai-assistant.md). Chat with it to manage your portfolio, research the market, strategize, and prepare your trades in plain language.

### How it works

When you're ready to trade, the assistant builds the transaction for you. It then hands you off to the [FatCat Web App](../../fatcat-web-app/fatcat-web-app.md), where you review, sign, and execute — nothing runs until you approve it.

You can also open the FatCat Web App directly in LINE at any time to trade, view charts, and manage your wallet.

{% hint style="info" %}
LINE never gets your seed phrase and never signs transactions — every trading action requires your approval.
{% endhint %}
