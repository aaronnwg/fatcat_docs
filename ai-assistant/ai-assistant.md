# AI Assistant

### Getting started

The AI Assistant is a conversational trading companion built into FatCat.

Ask it in plain language to research tokens, prepare trades, manage positions, explain features, and more.

It’s the easiest way to trade. No menus. No hunting for the right button. Just tell FatCat what you want.

### Features

#### What it does

* Pulls live prices, token stats, market data, your wallet balances, and more.
* Prepares swaps, Perp opens and closes, limit orders, DCA, stop-loss, take-profit, and transfers for you to review and sign.
* Edits existing positions and orders.
* Explains FatCat features, fees, and Solana trading basics.
* Works in English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

#### How it works

Every trade is prepared, not executed.

The assistant builds transactions and sends them to you for signing. Nothing moves without your approval.

It can see your wallet address, balances, positions, and chat history.

It cannot see your private keys or anyone else’s data.

To start a conversation, send a message to the bot on Telegram or tap the **AI Trade** bubble in the web app.

#### Limits and restrictions

* Trading only. Off-topic requests are refused.
* No financial advice. It shows data and prepares the trades you describe. It does not tell you what to buy or predict prices.
* Your account only. It cannot access other wallets or other users.
* No system internals.
* Rate limited. Per-user limits apply to message frequency and conversation length.
* A connected wallet is required for balances, positions, and trade prep.
